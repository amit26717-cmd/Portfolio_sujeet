About Me

Behind
The Camera

I'm Sujeet Patel, a photographer and visual storyteller interested in creating images that feel intentional, expressive and visually strong.

My work explores fashion, portraits, products, weddings, landscapes and creative visual concepts. I believe that photography is not only about capturing a moment, but also about creating a mood, telling a story and communicating an idea through light and composition.
